\skittitle{Emergency Broadcast System}

From: \url{https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-presentations/emergency-broadcast-system/}

Cub Scouts and Lion Partners stand in a line.  Narrator stands off to one side.\\
\\
Narrator: This is a test of the Emergency Broadcast System.  This is only a test.\\
\\
Cub Scouts and Lion Partners: “Beeeeeeeeeeep.” (continue until Narrator makes a hand signal.)\\
\\
Narrator: Thank you.  This was only a test.  Had this been a real emergency, you would have heard this:\\
\\
Cub Scouts and Lion Partners run around crazily, screaming.\\
