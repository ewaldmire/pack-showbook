\skittitle{Missing Tent}

From: \url{https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-presentations/missing-tent/}

Cast:\\
\\
Sherlock Holmes\\
\\
Dr. Watson\\
\\
 \\
\\
Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Watson lay on stage, staring upwards.\\
\\
Holmes: Well Watson, we’ve had a wonderful day of hiking and enjoying nature.\\
\\
Watson: Yes Holmes, we certainly have.\\
\\
Holmes: Look up.  What do you see?\\
\\
Watson: I see thousands of stars.\\
\\
Holmes: And what do you think that means?\\
\\
Watson: I think it means we will have another day of beautiful weather tomorrow.  What do you think it means?\\
\\
Holmes: I think it means someone has stolen our tent\\
\\
