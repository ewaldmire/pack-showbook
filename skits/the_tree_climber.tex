\skittitle{The Tree Climber}

From: \url{https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-presentations/the-tree-climber/}

Cast:\\
\\
Cub Scout 1\\
\\
Cub Scout 2\\
\\
Climber (offstage)\\
\\
 \\
\\
Tree climber is hidden offstage.\\
\\
Cub Scout 1: You know, they say there’s this really good tree climber trying out for the Olympics. I wonder if he’s practicing around here?\\
\\
Cub Scout 2: Call out and see!\\
\\
Cub Scout 1: Hey! Tree Climber! You around here?\\
\\
Climber: Yep!\\
\\
Cub Scout 1: You practicing?\\
\\
Climber: Yep!\\
\\
Cub Scout 1: How high are you?\\
\\
Climber: Oh, not high. About Cub Scout 100 feet.\\
\\
Cub Scout 1: Wow! Can you go higher?\\
\\
Climber: Yep! (pause) Now I’m at about 200 feet.\\
\\
Cub Scout 1: Fantastic! Can you go higher?\\
\\
Climber: Yep! (pause) Now I’m at about 275 feet.\\
\\
Cub Scout 1: Neato! Can you go higher?\\
\\
Climber: Yep! (pause) Now I’m at about 325 feet.\\
\\
Cub Scout 1: Great! Can you go higher?\\
\\
Climber: Yep! (pause) Now I’m at about 400 feet.\\
\\
Cub Scout 1: Gee! I’m amazed!\\
\\
Cub Scout 2: Excuse me, Sir, but I have a book here that says that the highest tree in the world is only 360 feet high!\\
\\
Climber: Ahhhhhh!!!!!! (Thump!)\\
