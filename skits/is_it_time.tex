\skittitle{Is It Time?}

From: \url{https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-presentations/is-it-time/}

Cub Scouts all sit on a bench, or row of chairs, right leg crossed over left.\\
\\
Cub Scout 1 to Cub Scout 2: Is it time?\\
\\
Cub Scout 2 to Cub Scout 3: Is it time?\\
\\
Cub Scout 3 to Cub Scout 4: Is it time?\\
\\
Continues down the line\\
\\
Final Cub Scout: No, it is not time.\\
\\
Message gets relayed back to Cub Scout 1.\\
\\
Repeat twice, but on the last time, Final Cub Scout: Yes, it is time.\\
\\
Message gets repeated down the line. All scouts look at each other, then cross left leg over right in unison.\\
