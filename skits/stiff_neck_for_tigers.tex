\skittitle{Stiff Neck (for Tigers)}

From: \url{https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-presentations/stiff-neck/}

Leader stands on stage, head far back.\\
\\
Cub Scout 1 and Adult Partner approach, look at the Leader, then look upwards.\\
\\
Cub Scout 2 and Adult Partner approach, and then stare upwards.\\
\\
Cub Scout 3 and Adult Partner approach, then stare upwards.\\
\\
Repeat until all but one Cub Scout is on stage, looking up.\\
\\
Final Cub Scout and Adult Partner enter, and ask what everyone’s looking at.\\
\\
Leader: I don’t know about these guys, but I just have a stiff neck.\\
