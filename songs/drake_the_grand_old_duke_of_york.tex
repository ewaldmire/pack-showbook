\songtitle{The Grand Old Duke of York}

\textit{Everyone in this song will need a chair}

\begin{verse}
The Grand Old Duke of York \\

He had ten thousand men \\

He marched them up the hill \textit{(stand up)} \\

And he marched them down again \textit{(sit down)} \\

And when they're up, they're up \textit{(stand up)} \\

And when they're down, they're down \textit{(sit down)} \\

And when they're only halfway up \textit{(stand halfway up)} \\

They're neither up nor down \textit{(stand up and down quickly)} \\
\end{verse}

\textit{You can speed up a bit each time you repeat it and see how fast everyone can go}

Fastest step will be: \\
Up - down - up - down - halfway up - up - down
