This is a repeat after me song \\
This is a do as I do song

Hi! my name is Joe \\

I got a wife and three kids, \\

and I'm working in a button factory. \\

One day my boss came to me. \\

He said "Joe, are you busy?" \\

I said, "No" \\

He said to "push this button with your right hand" \\

\textit{[make pushing motion with right hand.]} \\
\end{verse}
