\songtitle{Peel Bananas}
Motions: raise arms overhead one at a time to “form” the item, lower them one at a time to “shuck/slice/peel” it, then act out the final action. \\

From: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GYsJ6vQKFjo


\begin{verse}
So first you \\
(Quietly - raise 1 arm each time)\\
Form the corn, form, form the corn (and then you)\\
Form the corn, form, form the corn (and then you)\\

(Louder - lowering one arm each time)\\
shuck the corn, shuck, shuck the corn (and then you)\\
shuck the corn, shuck, shuck the corn (and then you)\\

(LOUD! Jump up and down hands up in the hair)\\
Pop the corn, pop, pop the corn! \quad (jump up and down)\\
Pop the corn, pop, pop the corn! \quad (jump up and down)\\
\end{verse}

\begin{verse}
(Quietly raise 1 arm each time)\\
Form potatoes, form, form potatoes \\
Form potatoes, form, form potatoes \\

(Louder - lowering one arm each time)\\
Slice potatoes, slice, slice potatoes \\
Slice potatoes, slice, slice potatoes \\

(LOUD! Stomp the potatoes to mash them!)\\
Mash potatoes, mash, mash potatoes! \quad (jump up and down, twisting) \\
Mash potatoes, mash, mash potatoes! \quad (jump up and down, twisting) \\
\end{verse}

\begin{verse}
(Quietly raise 1 arm each time)\\
Form bananas, form, form bananas \\
Form bananas, form, form bananas \\

(Louder - lowering one arm each time)\\
Peel bananas, peel, peel bananas \\
Peel bananas, peel, peel bananas \\

(LOUD! Stomp the potatoes to mash them!)\\
Go bananas, go, go bananas! \quad (spin around, waving arms wildly) \\
Go bananas, go, go bananas! \quad (spin around, waving arms wildly) \\
\end{verse}
