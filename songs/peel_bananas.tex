\songtitle{Peel Bananas}
Motions: raise arms overhead one at a time to “form” the item, lower them one at a time to “shuck/slice/peel” it, then act out the final action. \\

From: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GYsJ6vQKFjo


\begin{verse}
\textit{(Quietly - raise 1 arm each time in the shape of corn)}\\
...so first you \\
Form the corn, form, form the corn \quad (and then you)\\
Form the corn, form, form the corn \quad (and then you)\\
\bigskip
\textit{(Louder - lowering one arm each time)}\\
shuck the corn, shuck, shuck the corn \quad (and then you)\\
shuck the corn, shuck, shuck the corn \quad (and then you)\\
\bigskip
\textit{(LOUD! Jump up and down hands up in the hair)}\\
Pop the corn, pop, pop the corn! \quad (and then you)\\
Pop the corn, pop, pop the corn! \quad (and then you)\\
\end{verse}

\begin{verse}
\textit{(Quietly - raise 1 arm each time in the shape of a potatoes)}\\
Form potatoes, form, form potatoes \\
Form potatoes, form, form potatoes \\
\bigskip
\textit{(Louder - lowering one arm each time)}\\
Slice potatoes, slice, slice potatoes \\
Slice potatoes, slice, slice potatoes \\
\bigskip
\textit{(LOUD! Stomp the potatoes to mash them!)}\\
Mash potatoes, mash, mash potatoes!\\
Mash potatoes, mash, mash potatoes!\\
\end{verse}

\begin{verse}
\textit{(Quietly - raise 1 arm each time)}\\
Form bananas, form, form bananas \\
Form bananas, form, form bananas \\
\bigskip
\textit{(Louder - lowering one arm each time)}\\
Peel bananas, peel, peel bananas \\
Peel bananas, peel, peel bananas \\
\bigskip
\textit{(LOUD! Spin around and wave arms wildly!!)}\\
Go bananas, go, go bananas!\\
Go bananas, go, go bananas!\\
\end{verse}
