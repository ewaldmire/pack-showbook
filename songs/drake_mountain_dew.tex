\songtitle{Mountain Dew}

From: \url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Old_Mountain_Dew} \\

\begin{center}
\textit{Chorus:} \\
They call it that good old mountain dew, \\
And them that refuse it are few. (are few!) \\
I'll hush up my mug if you'll fill up my jug \\
With that good old mountain dew. \\
Mountain dew! \\
\end{center}

\begin{verse}
My aunt Lucille had an automobile, \\
It ran on a gallon or two. (or two!) \\
It didn't need no gas and it didn't need no oil, \\
It just ran on that good old mountain dew. Mountain dew! \\
\end{verse}

[Chorus]

\begin{verse}
My uncle Mort, he is sawed off and short, \\
He measures 'bout four foot two, (four foot two!) \\
But he thinks he's a giant when you give him a pint \\
Of that good old mountain dew. Mountain dew! \\
\end{verse}

[Chorus]

\begin{verse}
Old Auntie June had a brand new perfume, \\
It had such a wonderful "pee-ew" (pe-ew) \\
But to her surprise, when she had it analyzed, \\
It was nothing but that good old mountain dew. Mountain dew! \\
\end{verse}

[Chorus]

\begin{verse}
My uncle Klaus had a mean old mouse \\
When they asked how it happened, \\
He said it was a lappin' \\
That good old mountain dew \\
Mountain dew! \\
\end{verse}

[Chorus]

\begin{verse}
There's an old hollow tree, just a little way from me \\
Where you lay down a dollar or two (or two!) \\
If you hush up your mug, then they'll give you a jug \\
Of that good old mountain dew \\
\end{verse}

[Chorus]

\begin{verse}
Old Deacon Crane took a trip in the rain, \\
Said his wife had come down with the flu, (the flu!) \\
But she'll be all right if you give her a pint \\
Of that good old mountain dew. \\
\end{verse}

[Chorus]

\begin{verse}
You take a little trash and you mix it up with ash, \\
And you throw it in the sole of a shoe, (a shoe!) \\
Then you stir it awhile with an old rusty file, \\
And they call it that good old mountain dew. Mountain dew! \\
\end{verse}
