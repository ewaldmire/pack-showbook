\songtitle{The Great Chicago Fire}

This is a do what I do song

\begin{verse}
Late last night while we were all in bed \textit{(mime sleeping on hands)} \\

Old Ma O'Leary put Da lantern in da shed \textit{(mime hanging a lantern on a nail)} \\

and when da cow kicked it over \textit{(mime kicking)}, \\

She winked her eye and said \textit{(wink eye dramatically)} \\

"It's gonna be a hot time, in da old town, tonight." \textit{(mime fanning yourself from the heat)} \\

FIRE! FIRE! FIRE! \textit{(throw hands in the air with each "Fire")} \\
\end{verse}

\textit{Each verse you get quieter but continue to yell the fire part, make sure to continue with the movements to keep everyone informed where you are in the song!}

\textit{Repeat three times}
