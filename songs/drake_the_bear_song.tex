\songtitle{The Bear Song}

\textit{(Best sung with a flannel, fake beard, and/or axe)}

This is a repeat after me AND a sing together song

\begin{verse}
You repeat four lines back, \\
Then we will sing those 4 lines together \\
So everybody stand up! \\
\end{verse}

\begin{verse}
The other day \\
I saw a bear \\
A great big bear \\
Oh way out there \\
\textit{(Sing all together)} \\
\end{verse}

\begin{verse}
He looked at me \\
I looked at him \\
He sized up me \\
I sized up him \\
\textit{(Sing all together)} \\
\end{verse}

\begin{verse}
He said to me \\
Why don't you run \\
I see you ain't \\
Got any gun \\
\textit{(Sing all together)} \\
\end{verse}

\begin{verse}
And so I ran \\
Away from there \\
But right behind \\
Me was that bear \\
\textit{(Sing all together)} \\
\end{verse}

\begin{verse}
But up ahead \\
There was a tree \\
A great big tree \\
OH GLORY ME! \\
\textit{(Sing all together)} \\
\end{verse}

\begin{verse}
The lowest branch \\
Was 10 feet up \\
I'd have to jump \\
And trust my luck \\
\textit{(Sing all together)} \\
\end{verse}

\begin{verse}
And so I jumped \\
Into the air \\
But I missed that branch \\
Oh way up there \\
\textit{(Sing all together)} \\
\end{verse}

\begin{verse}
No don't you fret \\
And don't you frown \\
Cause I caught that branch \\
On the way back down \\
\textit{(Sing all together)} \\
\end{verse}

\begin{verse}
This is the end \\
There ain't no more \\
Unless I see \\
That bear once more \\
\textit{(Sing all together)} \\
\end{verse}
