\songtitle{The Pirate Song}

\begin{center}
\textit{Chorus:} \\
The day I went to sea. I climb aboard the pirate ship and the captain says to me we are going this way that way forward backward over the irish sea. A bottle of coke to soothe my throat and that's life for me yar yar yar yar \textit{*slight pause*} yar yar yar yar
\end{center}

\textbf{Actions:} \\
I climb aboard the pirate ship --- \textit{Act out climbing up a ladder} \\
the captain says to me --- \textit{(Salute)} \\
this way --- \textit{(lean left)} \\
that way --- \textit{(lean right)} \\
forward --- \textit{(lean forward)} \\
backward --- \textit{(lean backward)} \\
over the irish sea --- \textit{(Rolling motion with hands as leaning forward)} \\
A bottle of coke to soothe my throat --- \textit{(Drinking a beverage motion with hand *thumb and pinky as top and bottom)} \\
yar yar yar yar --- \textit{(Spin around while moving hand in a circle)} \\

\begin{verse}
When I was one I \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ (an action that rhymes with one) *Chorus* \\

When I was two I \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ (an action that rhymes with two) *Chorus* \\

When I was three I \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ (an action that rhymes with three) *Chorus* \\

When I was four there was no more \\
\end{verse}

\textbf{Example:}

\begin{verse}
When I was one I stepped on some gum *Chorus* \\

When I was two I got new shoes *Chorus* \\

When I was three I scraped my knee *Chorus* \\

When I was four there was no more \\
\end{verse}
